FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with new or small channels?

Yes. Small channels are my main focus. We start by looking at where your channel is now and what the next realistic milestone is.

What does Twitch promotion include?

Promoting your channel and streams to relevant audiences, plus suggestions for your channel presentation, categories, titles and schedule. The exact scope is agreed with you before we start.

What does YouTube SEO and optimization include?

A review of titles, descriptions, keywords, thumbnails, metadata and channel organization, with clear recommendations you can act on.

How do you report progress?

I compare your numbers before and after using the analytics each platform provides, and send you a short summary with screenshots.

Can you guarantee a specific number of viewers or subscribers?

No. Results depend on your content, niche, audience and platform changes. I can't guarantee a number, and I'd be wary of anyone who does.

What are your pricing options and delivery time frames?

It depends on the platform, the goal and the length of the work. Send a channel review request and I'll reply with a price and timeline for your case.

What access or information do you need to begin?

Your channel link and the goal you're working toward. For reporting, I'll ask you to share screenshots of your analytics. I don't need your password.
